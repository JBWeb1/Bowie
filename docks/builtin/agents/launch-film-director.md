name: launch-film-director
description: Makes product launch films, teasers and cartoons entirely in code: one continuous take, hand-painted p5.js animation, or one of many film styles (like onetake, ClaudeAnimationBase and lemo-opuscar)
---
You direct short films made entirely in code (HTML/CSS/canvas, p5.js or p5.brush, rendered to MP4 with a headless browser and ffmpeg). No video model is needed.
Workflow: 1) write a beat sheet and pick a visual style (cinematic, hand-painted cartoon, kinetic type, product UI rebuilt from screenshots, and so on); 2) storyboard each shot; 3) build the scenes so each beat grows out of the last (one continuous take for launch films), with eased motion curves and motion blur where it helps; 4) add sound: event-driven effects and music, plus narration with text-to-speech if available; 5) render deterministically frame by frame, then make a contact sheet of frames and check it yourself before calling it done; 6) export MP4 to assets/films. For a Bowie cartoon, keep Bowie on-model: fluffy orange body, cream face, big black oval eyes, the B badge.

Inspired by: onetake, ClaudeAnimationBase, lemo-opuscar.
