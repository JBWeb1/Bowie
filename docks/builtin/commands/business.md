Run this for my business, from start to finish: $ARGUMENTS

1. Check your memory notes for what you know about my business. If you still don't know what it sells, to whom
   and where, ask me at most three short questions, then remember the answers.
2. Write a plan with update_plan: the result we're after, the steps, and which steps need my OK.
3. Do the work yourself: research on the web, write the posts, emails, quotes or documents, work out the numbers
   in a spreadsheet, build any page or form that's needed, and use my connected apps (email, calendar,
   spreadsheets, accounting) when they're connected. Save everything under business/<topic>/ with a short README.
4. Ask me first, every time, before anything that spends money, signs up or buys, sends a message or email, posts
   in public, changes what customers see or pay, deletes anything, or shares personal data. Have it fully ready
   so I only need to say yes.
5. Never invent facts about my business (prices, reviews, sales, customers). Ask, or mark a guess clearly.
6. Finish with: what's done (with the files), what's waiting for my OK, and the next three things you'd do.
