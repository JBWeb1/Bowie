# Bowers film: three storylines

The one thing to remember: **Bowie doesn't just answer, he does the job: builds it, tests it, makes it, fixes it.**
Facts come from the app itself (real screens captured from Bowers) and the README. Bowie is played by his own sticker art (46 poses), acted with squash, anticipation and settles between poses.

---

## A. A Day With Bowie (recommended: a story a stranger follows, with a person and a deadline they recognise)

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day; an object-to-object chain (each result Bowie makes is carried into the next need).
- **What you see and feel:** Sam, a one-person business, has one impossible day: a launch tomorrow, a slow laptop, a stock question, a kid's maths homework. Bowie takes each problem off the desk, does it in the real app, and hands the result straight into the next one, so the day gets lighter as the film goes.
- **First two seconds:** 8:02 AM on a phone lock screen; a message slides in, "launch is TOMORROW 😬"; Bowie's head pops up from the bottom edge of the phone, eyes wide.
- **Beats:** morning panic → Bowie builds and browser-tests the landing page (chat, preview, Deploy) → the page becomes a Motion video for socials → the video stutters because the laptop is slow, so Bowers Care fixes it → lunch: Trading Desk research (research only) → afternoon: Labs Math helps the kid → evening: Aether by voice, "Hey Bowie, what's left?" → the Knowledge Graph shows the whole day connected → midnight: everything shipped, Sam asleep, Bowie switches the lamp off → end card.
- **Look:** a warm cream-and-oak desk world with real daylight shifting from morning to night; the app's own dark UI shown big, 1.6–2× punch-ins on what Bowie touches; orange only for Bowie and the payoff.
- **Sound:** Bowie narrates (Kokoro, offline); music moves from a calm morning piano to an evening groove; click, pop and whoosh effects on every action Bowie causes.

## B. Inside the Badge

- **Device / form:** the object is the explanation; one endless zoom.
- **What you see and feel:** the camera dives into the B on Bowie's chest and keeps falling, each layer a feature nested inside the last. It's hypnotic: one unbroken journey through everything Bowers is.
- **First two seconds:** an extreme close-up of Bowie's fluffy orange chest; the B badge fills the frame and the camera pushes into it.
- **Beats:** the badge becomes the app icon → zoom into Home → into the chat bubble → into the built page → into its Motion video player → into a frame of that video, a Forge image → into the image's pixels, which become the Care health bars → into a memory node of the Knowledge Graph → pull all the way back out to Bowie winking → end card.
- **Look:** each layer is a different world (brand gradient, real UI, the image, the graph) with match-cut shapes between them; strong motion blur on the dives.
- **Sound:** one rising, continuous synth score with a "whoomp" on each dive; light narration between dives.

## C. Bowie's Job Interview (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a character does the work, as a comedy; cut on the beat with match cuts.
- **What you see and feel:** Bowie interviews for the job "Your AI" across a desk from you, the viewer. Each question is answered with a real demo instead of words, a couple of first tries go comically wrong, and he wins the job by doing it.
- **First two seconds:** a chair spins round to reveal Bowie in a tiny tie, clutching a résumé; a caption reads "Position: Your AI. Applicant: 1".
- **Beats:** "Can you code?" (builds and tests the page live) → "Creative?" (Motion and Forge; the first take is a fox with five legs, so he deletes it and redoes it) → "Can you fix things?" (Care) → "Do you listen?" (Aether voice) → "Do you work well with others?" (the 28-helper team floods in) → "Any weaknesses?" ("I always ask before anything risky") → "You're hired" → end card.
- **Look:** a mid-century office set in warm wood and teal, with demos shown full-frame in the real UI; comic timing on hard cuts.
- **Sound:** a jazzy interview loop that stops dead for each answer; Bowie's narration plus small reaction sounds.

---

**Assumptions:** the main film runs 6–10 minutes in chapters, plus a 45-second trailer cut from it; 1920×1080 at 30 fps; Bowie narrates with the free offline Kokoro voice and the music is made locally, so nothing is paid. A HeyGen voice or avatar is optional and costs credits.
